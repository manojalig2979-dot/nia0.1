1. Our Core Principle: Architectural Sovereignty

At NDTechHub and NAVDIVA Group, we believe privacy cannot be achieved by mere corporate promises — it must be guaranteed by code and software architecture. Unlike legacy "computer use" agents that continuously record your monitor, screenshot private banking portals, and transmit raw frames to remote cloud servers, NIA 1.0 executes inside your local OS perimeter.

All automation tools (window focusing, volume management, AST code indexers, Word document generation) are Python libraries running natively on your local machine.

2. What We NEVER Collect or Transmit

Under no circumstances does NIA 1.0 or NDTechHub record, collect, sell, or proxy:

  • Display & Video Streams: Your display frames, desktop wallpaper, open browser tabs, or webcam streams are never broadcast to our servers.
  • Keystrokes & Passwords: We do not log keystrokes, password fields, or system clipboard buffers.
  • WhatsApp Chats & Media: Conversations handled via the persistent WhatsApp profile remain strictly between your desktop browser session and WhatsApp servers.
  • Code AST & Local Repositories: Code review plans and unified diffs generated in .nia_backups are 100% on-device.

3. Third-Party Model Gateways & BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)

NIA 1.0 operates on a Direct Gateway model. When you supply your Google Gemini API key or local runner endpoint:

  • Your API key is saved locally in encrypted application storage on your Windows PC.
  • In BYOK mode, requests travel directly from your desktop IP to Google AI Studio / your model provider. They never transit through an NDTechHub middleman server.
  • You retain 100% control over model retention settings according to Google's or your chosen provider's Terms of Service.

4. What Minimal Data Is Processed

To operate our commercial licensing and payment infrastructure, we only process:

Hardware Machine Hash

A non-reversible SHA-256 hash of your machine ID to prevent license sharing abuse.

Billing Email & License Key

Your email is collected during checkout to deliver license keys and verify active 7-day test flight periods.

5. Enterprise Air-Gapped Deployments

For defence, financial institutions, and enterprise engineering teams requiring zero outbound internet connectivity, NDTechHub delivers fully air-gapped NIA distributions. These package local Whisper weights, local quantized LLMs (e.g. Llama 3 / Mistral via vLLM), and offline Three.js avatar assets.
